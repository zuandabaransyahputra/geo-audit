# 0006. Sources and scope of the training opt-out

Status: accepted (2026-11-23)

## Decision

- Sources, in order of precedence: the request (`?training=optout`, `--training-optout`) and then
  `Content-Signal: ai-train=no` that applies to all crawlers (a `User-agent: *` group or a line before any group). A
  signal in a bot-specific group does not turn it on. `Report.options.trainingOptOutSource` records which one applied.
- The opt-out never applies when robots.txt is unreachable or 5xx: then everything is blocked for a different reason.
- Training products become `opted-out` only when robots.txt blocks their crawlers; a 404 page still makes them blocked.
- Inside answer products, blocked training-only crawlers are still named in the reason ("opted out on purpose") but no
  longer lower the status.
- Without the opt-out, ai-training-bots has effort `hours` (never a quick win) and the note "Only if you want models to
  learn from the site".
- Share links and the Markdown "Generated by" link carry `&training=optout` only when the opt-out came from the request.
  A Content-Signal opt-out is found again on every audit.
- The OG card signs an extra `t` flag and shows "Training crawlers opted out on purpose".
